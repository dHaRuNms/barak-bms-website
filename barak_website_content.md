### Product 1: Clara Bot — AI Technical & Operational Assistant

#### Features & Benefits

---

### Product 2: Open-Source EDA & PCB Design Software Tools

#### Overview
Democratizing hardware development through robust, open-source Electronic Design Automation (EDA) and PCB layout utilities.

#### Features & Benefits
* **Developer-Centric Tooling:** Streamlines schematic capture, PCB layout validation, and component library management.
* **Rapid Prototyping Ecosystem:** Accelerates hardware design iterations for electronics engineers and researchers.
* **Community-Driven Flexibility:** Customizable workflows without proprietary vendor lock-in.

---

### Product 3: Battery Emulator & Digital Simulator Software

#### Overview
A digital twin simulation suite that allows engineers to model, simulate, and test battery packs dynamically under real-world stress conditions before physical deployment.

#### Features & Benefits
* **Virtual Battery Emulation:** Simulates dynamic charge/discharge profiles, temperature variations, and cell degradation modes.
* **Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Integration:** Enables safe validation of controller algorithms without risking real battery packs.
* **Accelerated Testing:** Reduces R&D testing timelines and cost-to-market significantly.

---

### Product 4: Commercial Ride-Hailing & Fleet Management App

#### Overview
A scalable, real-time mobility application suite designed for electric urban transit networks, auto-rickshaw fleets, and on-demand commercial rides.

#### Features & Benefits
* **Real-Time Dispatch & Routing:** High-accuracy location tracking, dynamic route calculation, and seamless rider-driver matching.
* **Fleet Telemetry Integration:** Syncs directly with vehicle instrumentation and battery monitoring systems.
* **Secure Payment & Verification:** Integrated OTP/SMS verification, driver management, and passenger booking flows.
